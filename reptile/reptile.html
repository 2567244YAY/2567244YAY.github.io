Welcome to Reptiles in North East Scotland

This interactive map visualises verified reptile sightings across North East Scotland between 2005 and 2025. It allows you to explore how species may be distributed across the region, how patterns change over time, and where reptile activity is most concentrated.

How to explore the map

  • Use the Species Filters to show or hide individual reptile species.
  • Adjust the Year Slider to explore how sightings change through time.
  • Toggle the Heatmap to reveal hotspots and areas of higher reptile activity.
  • View the Species Density Widget on the right to see real‑time counts of visible species based on your filters and selected year.
  • Hover over a point to see a species photo and quick details.
  • Click a point to view the full record information, including locality and taxonomy.
  • Use the search bar to locate places across the region.
  • Close (🦎), hide (🐍) and open (🐢) the side panels to focus on the map or explore additional information and features.

Tip: Combine the heatmap, species filters, and year slider to reveal ecological patterns, hotspots, and unusual sightings across the landscape.
